隐私政策
Privacy Policy

更新于 2026 年 10 月 9 日 · Updated October 9, 2026

你决定何时拍照、选择照片，以及是否允许处理。

照片用于妆容预览和指导，不用于识别你的身份。

Apple 处理付款；Mirror 不接收银行卡或 Apple 账户密码。

Photos / 照片

经你同意后，Mirror 仅将你选择的照片发送到后端及 OpenAI，用于分析妆容、生成预览和指导，不用于身份识别。正式服务的临时文件在请求结束后删除。App 不保存结果历史；你可以主动保存或分享结果。

OpenAI 可能按其政策保留 API 数据；删除后端文件并不代表删除供应商记录。你可以在 App 中撤回同意，停止后续上传。

Mirror sends only the photo you select to its backend and OpenAI after your consent, to analyze makeup, generate a preview, and prepare guidance. Photos are not used to identify you. Processing files are temporary on the production server and removed after the request. The app does not keep a result history; you can choose to save or share results yourself.

OpenAI may retain API data under its own policies; server file deletion does not delete provider records. See OpenAI data controls. You can withdraw consent in the app to stop future uploads.

Subscriptions and usage / 订阅与使用记录

Apple 处理付款，Mirror 不获取你的 Apple 账户密码、邮箱或银行卡。后端保存经 Apple 验证的订阅标识、产品、到期时间、试用状态和每日次数，以验证权益和恢复购买。订阅记录目前由运营者删除；每日次数和请求记录两天后过期。指导文字缓存最长一小时，不含照片数据。App 在设备钥匙串中保存购买凭证，并在设备上保存偏好。

Apple processes payments. Mirror does not receive your Apple Account password, email, or payment card. The backend stores signed-transaction-derived subscription identifiers, product, expiry, trial status, and daily usage to verify access and restore purchases. A secret-derived identifier links allowance use across devices. Subscription records currently remain until removed by the operator; daily quota/request records expire after two days. Guidance text is cached for up to one hour, without photo bytes. The app stores purchase proof in the device Keychain and preferences on your device.

Service providers / 服务供应商

Vercel 提供服务托管，Upstash 保存订阅及使用记录，OpenAI 处理所选照片。基础设施供应商可能保留 IP 等运行日志。Mirror 不包含广告追踪，也不出售照片。

Vercel hosts the service, Upstash stores subscription and usage records, and OpenAI processes selected photos. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs such as IP addresses. Mirror does not include advertising trackers or sell your photos.

Contact / 联系

隐私请求请联系开发者。请勿在公开问题中发布照片、购买凭证或其他私人信息。公开上架前将复核联系渠道及保留政策。

For privacy requests, contact the developer. Do not post photos, receipts, or other private information in public issues. This contact channel and retention policy will be reviewed before public release.

相机与照片权限 / Camera and photo permissions

相机仅在你点击拍照时启用。拍摄的照片先留在设备内存中，不会自动保存到相册或上传。你确认照片并同意处理后，生成操作才会上传。相册使用系统选择器，App 不请求整个照片库的访问权限。

The camera opens only when you choose to take a photo. Captures stay in device memory and are not automatically saved or uploaded. Upload happens when you consent and request generation. The system photo picker shares selected photos without granting access to your entire library.
