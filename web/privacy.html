1. Who operates Mirror and what this policy covers运营者与适用范围

Mirror: Makeup Try-On & Guide is operated by Bo Zhang. This policy applies to the Mirror iPhone app and the production website at mirror-makeup.vercel.app, including invitation-only beta testing. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to maybe35@163.com.

Mirror 由 Bo Zhang 运营。本政策适用于 iPhone App、线上网站及受邀测试。隐私请求请发送至 maybe35@163.com。

2. Photos, facial processing, and AI guidance照片、面部处理与 AI 指导

You choose which photo to use. The camera is accessed only when you choose to take a photo. The iPhone system photo picker gives the app access to selected photos, rather than your entire photo library. Selecting or taking a photo does not by itself start AI processing. After you permit processing and request generation, a working copy is sent to Mirror’s backend and OpenAI to analyze visible makeup, generate a makeup preview, and check the result. When you tap Like to request guidance, the original and generated working images are sent again to prepare the comparison, annotations, makeup instructions, and styling suggestions.

Processing uses facial landmarks and feature regions to place cosmetic changes and check that facial geometry is preserved. Mirror does not use this processing to recognize or authenticate your identity, build a face-recognition database, or compare you with other people. Production image processing removes embedded photo metadata, including EXIF/GPS information, from the working image. This does not make visible people or surroundings in the photo anonymous.

Mirror does not use your photos to train its own AI models or sell them. OpenAI’s handling of API inputs and outputs is governed by its applicable policies and our account configuration. We do not promise zero retention by OpenAI. See OpenAI API data controls.

仅在你允许处理并点击生成后，工作图才发送给 Mirror 后端和 OpenAI；点击喜欢后，前后图片会再次用于核对与教学。面部关键点用于妆容定位及形状检查，不用于身份识别、认证或人脸数据库。工作图会去除 EXIF/GPS 等元数据，但照片中可见的内容仍可能识别个人。Mirror 不出售照片，也不使用照片训练自己的模型；OpenAI 的保留政策及账户配置另行适用。

3. Optional location and weather可选位置与天气

If you grant location permission, the app obtains a location through your device’s location service and rounds coordinates to one decimal place before sending them directly to MET Norway for weather. This is a coarse area, approximately 11 km in latitude; longitude distance varies by location. Mirror’s backend and OpenAI receive only the temperature and weather conditions used for styling, not those coordinates. The app may temporarily cache weather in memory.

MET Norway receives your IP address and the rounded coordinates in the weather request and may retain them in access logs. See MET Norway’s API terms and personal-data information. Declining or withdrawing location permission, or a weather-service failure, does not prevent makeup generation or weather-free styling suggestions. Weather data is provided by MET Norway under CC BY 4.0 and summarized for styling.

定位完全可选。坐标在设备上取整至小数点后一位后直接发送给 MET Norway；南北方向约 11 公里，东西方向随纬度变化。Mirror 后端和 OpenAI 仅接收温度及天气状况。天气供应商可记录粗略坐标和 IP。拒绝定位不影响妆容功能。

4. Purchases, invitations, and usage records购买、邀请与次数记录

You do not need to create a Mirror account for the current beta. Apple handles App Store purchases where purchase features are available. Mirror does not automatically obtain your Apple Account email address, password, or payment-card details. To verify access and restore purchases, the backend processes Apple-verified transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription status, expiry, trial status, and usage counts. A secret-derived identifier links verified access and usage across devices.

For free beta access, the backend verifies your private invitation code and stores a code-derived identifier, its allowance, activation/expiry time, and usage/request records. A code is not automatically linked to an email address; people sharing one code share its allowance. The developer’s private issuance log records issued codes, their creation times, allowances, and validity periods. If you request an invitation by email, LinkedIn, or TestFlight, the contact details you provide may also be used to send the invitation and respond to you.

Apple 处理购买，Mirror 不会自动获取 Apple 账户邮箱、密码或银行卡。后端处理经验证的交易标识、产品、状态及次数。免费测试记录与测试码关联，不自动绑定邮箱；同码共享额度。你主动提供的邮箱或联系信息可用于发送邀请、回复反馈和隐私请求。

5. On-device storage, cookies, and technical data设备存储、Cookie 与技术信息

The app stores access-session tokens and purchase proof in the iOS Keychain and saves preferences, including photo-processing consent and language, on the device. The website uses local storage for language and a signed, secure, HttpOnly cookie for anonymous allowance and request validation. These are functional storage mechanisms, not advertising trackers.

Hosting and service providers necessarily process network information such as IP addresses, request times, and operational/error logs. Mirror’s application-level access records use secret-derived identifiers, event times, request/job identifiers, and outcome information to enforce limits and diagnose errors. Do not include photos, private invitation codes, or purchase proofs in public feedback.

设备钥匙串保存权限会话及购买凭证，偏好保存在设备上。网页使用功能性语言存储及匿名额度 Cookie，不作广告追踪。基础设施会处理 IP、请求时间及运行日志；应用事件记录使用派生标识、请求标识与结果信息。

6. How long data is retained保留期限

On the Vercel production service, temporary uploaded photos, generated images, facial-processing files, and processing artifacts are normally cleaned up when each generation or guidance request ends. They are not added to a permanent photo library. Photos and results remain available in the current app or browser session; Mirror does not provide a result-history recovery service. Copies you deliberately save or share are controlled by you and the receiving service.

  • Anonymous and subscription daily quota/request records generally expire after about two days.
  • Application access events without photo bytes are retained for up to 31 days.
  • Guidance text and continuation metadata are cached or valid for up to one hour; the cache does not contain photo bytes.
  • Beta request/replay markers expire after up to 31 days. Beta activation, allowance, and usage-counter records currently remain until removed by the operator, even after access expires.
  • Subscription entitlement records and the developer’s private invitation-issuance log currently remain until removed by the operator. Contact us to request review or deletion of records that can be associated with you.
  • Support messages and voluntarily supplied feedback are retained as needed to respond, investigate issues, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Provider logs and OpenAI records have separate retention rules. Removing Mirror’s temporary files does not delete provider-held records. Local development tests may retain diagnostic photos and reports until the developer deletes them; this is separate from the production app’s request cleanup.

线上服务通常在每次请求结束时清理临时照片与处理文件，不提供历史结果恢复。每日额度及请求记录约两天；无照片的访问事件最多 31 天；教学缓存最长一小时；测试请求标记最多 31 天。测试激活、使用计数、订阅权益及私下发码记录目前需由运营者删除，过期不代表这些记录已删除。供应商日志及 OpenAI 记录另有保留规则。本地开发测试可能保留诊断文件直至开发者删除。

7. Service providers and international processing供应商与跨境处理

Mirror uses OpenAI for AI photo processing and guidance, Vercel for hosting, Upstash for access and usage records, Apple for platform permissions, TestFlight distribution and purchases, and MET Norway for optional weather. These providers receive the data necessary for their role. The app does not send your photo to the weather provider. Mirror does not include advertising trackers or sell your personal information.

Data may be processed outside Quebec or Canada, including in countries where service providers operate. Privacy protections and lawful access requirements may differ from those in your location. Providers’ applicable privacy policies also describe their own processing: OpenAI, Vercel, Upstash, Apple, and MET Norway.

供应商包括 OpenAI、Vercel、Upstash、Apple 和可选天气供应商 MET Norway，各自仅按服务角色处理所需数据。天气供应商不会收到照片。数据可能在魁北克或加拿大境外处理，供应商所在地的隐私和依法访问规则可能不同。Mirror 不出售个人信息，也不包含广告追踪。

8. Your choices and privacy requests你的选择与隐私请求

You can choose not to upload a photo, decline optional location access, change camera or location permissions in iOS Settings, and withdraw photo-processing consent in the app’s Privacy screen. Withdrawing consent stops future uploads; it cannot recall a request already sent or automatically erase records held by providers. You can leave beta access from the access screen. Clearing browser storage removes local preferences and cookies but does not necessarily delete server records. Files you saved or shared must be managed separately.

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, or raise a privacy concern. Email maybe35@163.com with enough information to locate the relevant record. We may need to verify your request and may retain information required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Because Mirror does not maintain a photo history or a conventional user account, we may not be able to identify a historical photo or link an anonymous record to you. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive photos with an initial request.

可在 App 中撤回后续照片处理同意，在 iOS 设置中更改相机和定位权限，也可退出测试权限。撤回不能召回已发出的请求或自动删除供应商记录。你可通过邮箱请求查询、更正或删除可关联到你的个人信息；可能需要核实身份，部分记录依法或为安全目的需保留。没有照片历史或普通账户时，未必能定位某张历史照片或匿名记录。

9. Security and appropriate use安全与适当使用

Production network requests use HTTPS. Access tokens, signed invitation codes, server-side verification, and usage limits help protect access. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep invitation codes private and upload only photos you have permission to use. Mirror is a cosmetic visualization and guidance service, not a medical service. It is not directed to children under 13; if you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us so we can investigate.

线上请求使用 HTTPS，并采用权限会话、签名及额度控制；任何系统都不能保证绝对安全。请保密测试码，只上传有权使用的照片。本产品用于妆容展示及指导，不是医疗服务，也不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
