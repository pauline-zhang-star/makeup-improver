How can we help?
使用帮助

Contact maybe35@163.com for help with Mirror. Include your app version, iPhone model, and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, purchase receipts, private invitation codes, or sensitive photos.

需要帮助请联系上述邮箱，说明 App 版本、手机型号及问题。请勿发送密码、购买凭证、私人邀请码或敏感照片。

Try a makeup look 生成妆容

Take or choose a clear, front-facing photo in even lighting. Choose a style and tap Generate. Keep Mirror open while processing. AI results can vary; try another style or photo if a result does not suit you.

拍摄或选择光线均匀、五官清晰的正面照，选择风格后点击生成。处理时保持 App 打开，效果不合适时可更换风格或照片。

Compare and learn 对比与学习

Like an accepted preview to open the before-and-after comparison and makeup guidance. Numbers on the generated photo correspond to the instructions below. Complete Your Look provides complementary styling ideas; location is optional and adds local weather when available.

喜欢生成效果后，可查看前后对比及图中编号对应的技法说明。Complete Your Look 提供搭配建议，可选定位用于结合当地天气。

Subscriptions and daily allowance 订阅与次数

Basic, Plus, and Premium provide up to 1, 2, and 3 generations per day respectively. Daily allowance resets at midnight UTC and does not roll over. Once AI processing begins, that generation counts even if the result cannot be accepted. Guidance for an accepted look does not use another generation.

If an active purchase is missing, open Membership and use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to purchase. Restoring a purchase does not reset used generations. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. For refunds, visit Apple’s Report a Problem.

三档每天分别最多生成 1／2／3 次，UTC 零点重置，不结转。AI 开始处理后即计一次，即使效果未被接受；教学不另扣次数。可在会员页恢复购买，恢复不会重置已用次数。管理或取消订阅请前往 iPhone 设置中的 Apple 账户订阅页面，退款由 Apple 处理。

Connection or processing problems 网络与处理问题

Check your internet connection and try again. If a request appears stuck, avoid repeatedly tapping Generate. Contact support with the time of the issue and any error message so we can investigate. Mirror does not offer a permanent photo or result history; save any results you want to keep.

Privacy 隐私

Read our Privacy Policy for photo processing, optional location, retention, and privacy requests. You can withdraw future photo-processing consent in the app’s Privacy screen and change camera or location permission in iOS Settings.
